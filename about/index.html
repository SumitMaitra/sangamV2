About the Conference

SANGAM 2027

SANGAM 2027 marks a significant step towards strengthening academic collaboration, interdisciplinary research, and scholarly engagement among the National Institutes of Technology (NITs). Evolving from the legacy of MDRC, SANGAM will serve as a recurring PAN-NIT academic platform from 2027 onwards, bringing together faculty, researchers, and doctoral scholars to exchange ideas and advance research in management and social sciences.

SANGAM symbolizes the coming together of institutions, disciplines, ideas, and perspectives. The conference aims to foster inter-institutional research collaboration, joint publications, doctoral mentorship, and knowledge exchange while promoting academic excellence and a shared scholarly identity among participating NITs.

SANGAM brings together nine institutions: NIT Agartala, NIT Bhopal, NIT Durgapur, NIT Jamshedpur, NIT Meghalaya, NIT Patna, NIT Rourkela, NIT Silchar and NIT Warangal.

The inaugural edition, SANGAM 2027, will be hosted by the National Institute of Technology Durgapur in February 2027, laying the foundation for a sustainable PAN-NIT academic network committed to research excellence, institutional cooperation, and intellectual exchange.

About NIT Durgapur

Established in 1960, National Institute of Technology Durgapur (NITDGP) is a premier Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Part of the prestigious NIT system, NITDGP is renowned for excellence in engineering, science, and management education. Located in the industrial city of Durgapur, West Bengal, it boasts a vibrant campus with modern infrastructure, cutting-edge laboratories, innovation hubs, and a dynamic student community drawn from across the country. With a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary research and industry-aligned learning, the institute upholds its motto: “Excellence in Engineering Education and Research”. NIT Durgapur offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines, fostering a culture of innovation and critical thinking.

About DMS

The Department of Management Studies (DMS), National Institute of Technology Durgapur, is committed to advancing excellence in management education, research and scholarly engagement. Situated within the multidisciplinary and technology-oriented academic environment of NIT Durgapur, the Department provides a distinctive platform for the study and practice of contemporary management. The Department, established in 2004, offers MBA, Ph.D., and M.Tech in OR programs, with specializations in Marketing, Finance, HRM, Systems & Operations.

The Department emphasizes the integration of management knowledge with technology, analytics, innovation and interdisciplinary perspectives, reflecting the changing requirements of organizations and society. Through its academic programmes, research activities and scholarly initiatives, DMS seeks to develop a strong understanding of contemporary business and management challenges.
