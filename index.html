About the Conference

SANGAM 2027

SANGAM 2027 marks a significant step towards strengthening academic collaboration, interdisciplinary research, and scholarly engagement among the National Institutes of Technology (NITs). Evolving from the legacy of MDRC, SANGAM will serve as a recurring PAN-NIT academic platform from 2027 onwards, bringing together faculty, researchers, and doctoral scholars to exchange ideas and advance research in management and social sciences.

SANGAM brings together nine institutions: NIT Agartala, NIT Bhopal, NIT Durgapur, NIT Jamshedpur, NIT Meghalaya, NIT Patna, NIT Rourkela, NIT Silchar and NIT Warangal.
