Submission Guidelines

  • All submissions will be peer-reviewed for relevance, originality, clarity, and academic contribution.
  • All submissions shall be formatted in Times New Roman, with double line spacing. The heading shall be in 14-point font, while the body text shall be in 12-point font. The APA (7th Edition) style of referencing shall be used for the submission of the paper.
  • Authors may submit their abstracts/extended abstract within 800 words or a full paper (including all the figures and tables) within 8,000 words, which shall cover the following sections (not limited to):
    1. Introduction
    2. Literature Review
    3. Study design/methodology/approach
    4. Findings
    5. Conclusion
  • Each submission shall include at least four (04) unique keywords and JEL classification numbers. Submissions must be kept free from the authors’ identification details for blind peer review.
  • Only selected full papers will be considered for publication in a Scopus-indexed journal, and a few selected papers may be published in the e-journal NITI: Journal of Management Research, Department of Management Studies, NIT Durgapur. Following the submission, all manuscripts will undergo peer review in accordance with the journal’s established policies and procedures.
  • List of Journals (Scopus-indexed) will be shared later.
  • All accepted papers shall be published in conference proceedings with ISBN number.
